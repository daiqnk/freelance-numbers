Landlord Mileage Deduction: 2025 and 2026 IRS Rates and a Calculator

Checked 2026-10-16 against IRS Publication 527 (2025) and the other IRS pages listed under Sources. US federal rules only. How we make content

Trips to collect rent, show a vacancy, or buy supplies for a repair can be deductible rental expenses. Trips from your home to the rental usually count as commuting unless your home is your principal place of business. Here are the rules and the current rates.

What counts

Pub. 527: you may be able to deduct ordinary and necessary local transportation expenses incurred to collect rental income or to manage, conserve, or maintain the rental property. Travel between your home and a rental property is generally nondeductible commuting unless your home is your principal place of business (Pub. 587 covers that test).

You can use either actual expenses or the standard mileage rate, and you need records that meet the rules in chapter 5 of Pub. 463. Pub. 527 also says you must complete Form 4562, Part V, to deduct car expenses under either method.

A simple log

For each trip write the date, start and end point, miles and purpose ("collect rent", "meet plumber"). Split the year at June 30 for 2026 so each half uses its own rate. Auto and travel costs go on Schedule E line 6.

Quick answers

What is the IRS mileage rate for 2026?

72.5 cents per mile for business miles driven January 1 to June 30, 2026, and 76 cents per mile from July 1 to December 31, 2026, per the IRS standard mileage rates page.

Can landlords deduct driving to their rental property?

Pub. 527 allows ordinary and necessary local transportation to collect rent or manage, conserve or maintain the property, but trips from home to the rental are generally commuting unless home is your principal place of business.
