How much to charge as a freelance bookkeeper

Wage data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2025 (SOC 43-3031), retrieved 2026-10-16. Our calculations and assumptions are labelled. How we make content

Employed bookkeeping, accounting and auditing clerks in the US earn a median $24.36 an hour ($50,670 a year), according to the BLS. A freelancer who wants the same pay has to cover unbilled hours, business costs and the employer half of payroll taxes, which with the assumptions below works out to roughly $47.96 an hour, about 2.0× the employee median.

* Hourly × 2,080, the full-time year the BLS uses for annual figures. The category covers people who record financial transactions, update records and check them for accuracy. OEWS counts wage and salary employees only; it does not survey the self-employed, and its wages exclude benefits. So this is a benchmark for what an employer would pay, not a survey of freelance rates.

From employee wage to freelance rate

An employee is paid for about 2,080 hours a year. A freelancer only bills some of their hours; the rest goes to finding clients, admin, invoicing and unpaid gaps. Our formula:

hourly rate = (target income × 1.0648 + business costs + benefits you fund)
              ÷ billable hours ÷ (1 − payment fee %)
  • 1.0648: self-employment tax is 15.3% (IRS), applied to 92.35% of net earnings, which is 14.13%; an employee pays 7.65% themselves, so the extra is about 6.48%. This is an approximation; it ignores the Social Security wage cap and the income-tax deduction for half of SE tax.
  • Business costs: we use $6,000/year as an example (software, equipment, insurance, accounting). This is our assumption, not a statistic. Use your own.
  • Billable hours: we show 1,000, 1,250 and 1,500 hours a year as scenarios. Again, assumptions: count your own.

Turning the hourly floor into quotes

Many freelance bookkeeping, accounting and auditing clerks price monthly fixed fee based on transaction volume and number of accounts, with catch-up work quoted separately. Whatever the unit, estimate the hours behind it and check the quote against your floor with the project quote calculator.

Tip: Price by monthly transaction volume and accounts reconciled, and quote clean-up of messy back months as a separate project.
