Etsy fees on a $10 item

Fee rates checked 2026-10-16 against Etsy's Help Center (US sellers, Etsy Payments). Not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy, Inc. How we make content

On a $10 item with free shipping, a US seller pays $1.40 in Etsy fees, or 14.0% of the sale, and receives $8.60 before materials, packaging, postage and time. Here is the line-by-line math, what changes with shipping or Offsite Ads, and a calculator for your own numbers.

Fee breakdown: $10 item, free shipping

FeeHow it's calculatedAmount
Listing fee$0.20 per listing$0.20
Transaction fee6.5% × $10.00$0.65
Payment processing3% × $10.00 + $0.25$0.55
Total Etsy fees14.0% of the sale$1.40
You receive$10.00 − $1.40$8.60

Each fee is rounded to the cent. The two flat parts ($0.20 listing + $0.25 processing) are 4.5% of a $10 price, which is why fees bite much harder on cheap items.

Same item, different situations

ScenarioBuyer pays youEtsy feesYou receive

Buyer pays $5 shipping$15.00$1.88$13.12
Sale from Offsite Ads, 15% rate$10.00$2.90$7.10
Sale from Offsite Ads, 12% rate$10.00$2.60$7.40
Two of this item in one order$20.00$2.55 ($1.28 per item)$17.45
  • Shipping: the 6.5% transaction fee and processing fee also apply to what the buyer pays for shipping, so the $5 adds $0.48 in fees. The postage you buy is a separate cost.
  • Offsite Ads: the 15% rate applies to shops that have always made less than $10,000 in any 365 days; shops above that pay 12% and can't opt out. The fee never exceeds $100 per order.
  • Two in one order: you pay $0.20 for each additional quantity sold, but the $0.25 processing flat fee only once per order.
  • Sales tax: Etsy says processing fees are taken from the total sale price including applicable sales tax, so a taxed order costs a little more (3% of the tax amount). Enter the tax in the calculator to include it.

Quick answers

How much does Etsy take from a $10 sale?

For a US seller using Etsy Payments with free shipping and no Offsite Ads, fees are $1.40: $0.20 listing, $0.65 transaction (6.5%) and $0.55 processing (3% + $0.25). You receive $8.60 before your own costs.

What if a $10 sale comes from Offsite Ads?

Add $1.50 at the 15% rate (shops under $10,000 in yearly sales) or $1.20 at 12%, bringing total fees to $2.90 or $2.60.
