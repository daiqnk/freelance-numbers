The fees used

  • Listing: $0.20 per listing (and per additional quantity sold)
  • Transaction: 6.5% of item price plus shipping and gift wrap
  • Payment processing (US): 3% + $0.25 per order, on the total including shipping and applicable sales tax
  • Offsite Ads (only on attributed orders): 15%, or 12% for shops at $10,000+ in 365 days, capped at $100 per order

For how these fees fit into full profit per item, read the Etsy fee and profit guide.
